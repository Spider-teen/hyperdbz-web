This is a non profit fan project, started in 2014 (actually, earlier than that).
This version includes all of the work we developed these last eleven years. We would like to thank everyone that supported us and joined us over the course of this period for being part of this experiment.

HDBZ is a non-profit fanwork created as a concept game, a Capcom-styled Dragonball fighting game to be specific. Every single bit in the game was created by fans for fans, with us trying to create a unified experience. 
We don't own Dragonball in any way and we ask you to please support all official releases of it.
This game should not be hosted on any site that charges money to either download it or participate on the site. 
There should never be any money exchange connected to this game (we don't even use ads on our youtube videos or sites).

The artists involved might take donations themselves, in case you want to check those out please refer to our readme which has all the credits and corresponding links. 
None of that goes towards the development of this game, as the team making this is a continuously evolving group with people joining and leaving.

In order to evolve this project, we encourage people to join or contribute your time and talent with the core group trying to keep the feel and quality ongoing, 
and we appreciate every bit of support we ever got, even if just as enthusiasts showing it off to their friends. You rock!

Going more in-depth about HDBZ and how we got here...
In 2014, Eleven years before this release, a small group of MUGEN devs were making Dragonball characters,
experimenting with styles and gameplay, and after making a couple characters we decided, mostly as a joke but also as a concept, 
to put together a Youtube video portraying what a whole game made in those systems would look like if it was real.
The result was this video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZzQkB1gVtXA

This featured playable versions of just Goku, Vegeta and Frieza.
It was a wild success, people loved our vision and that inspired us to keep going and putting together more of this concept.

Down the line we released Gohan and a joke version of Mr. Satan.
Even later, Super Saiyan Goku, Babidi and Majin Vegeta joined the fray.
Eighteen, Piccolo, Candy Vegetto, Super Buu and Gotenks followed suit, along with an unannounced 'true' version of Mr. Satan. This build was dubbed the CHAMP edition.

By the time Dragonball Super was out, we put out an update that added bits from it to the characters and updated them further, 
allowing SSJ Goku and SSJ Vegeta to transform into their Super Saiyan Blue forms (after first experimenting a bit with the Cyans as boss versions).
Accompanying them were Tenshinhan and an unannounced Pilaf (dubbed the INDIGO edition, by now we were naming the builds for their secret characters).

And now, in 2025 comes the WICKED ambition...Jiren, Black and the unannounced character that dubs this version are on this final version. 
The final version for MUGEN, where we pushed all we could reach with this engine.
That means an Arcade intro, endings for every character, and all the references we could force down the coders' capable hands.

It was a wild ride and all of our lifes took turns over these years. Some of us married and some of us got kids, we went from teens to full grown adults (mostly). 

Returning to this for the fun of making something that ignites passion into people, HDBZ has been a passion project through and through 
and personally I have met and worked with people I consider great friends who I would otherwise never have met.

Right now, we are thinking of making an eventual transfer to the IKEMEN engine, as it is bursting with potential. 

We are a group of diverse people from all around the world, from all sorts of cultures who gathered under one single thing, our love for the work of Toriyama and fighting games. 
Toriyama has passed away, but he marked all of our lives with his passion.
He was a fantastic artist who liked raunchy jokes ,poo on a stick and gag manga, and he ended up redefining what magic meant for all the generations that come after him.
He made children believe that even if they were a nobody they could soar.
If tomorrow a voice come out of the sky begging you to raise your hands and share your energy, you would do it, no matter your age, because you grew in a world he transformed. and that's special. almost as special as balancing a dino on a ball.
In loving respect, he changed everyone of us for the better.


Whatever happens, it's great to have you and to show you what we managed to create, with passion, nerdiness and that feeling that a bit of effort goes a long way into showing what could be.


For all of us- past members, present, and future, we thank you for playing.
